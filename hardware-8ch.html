BrainWave 8-Channel EEG

An 8-channel research-grade electroencephalography system configured to capture bilateral temporal and articulatory motor activation before signal attenuation.

Engineering Scope Note: This unit operates in a verified 8-channel configuration. High-density montages (such as 128 channels) represent long-term research trajectories and are not present on physical test benches.

CHANNEL COUNT 8 Channels Active differential electrodes (F3, C3, T3, Pz, F4, C4, T4, Oz)
SAMPLING RATE 1,000 Hz / Channel Independent per-channel simultaneous sampling
INPUT RESOLUTION 24-Bit Sigma-Delta Ultra-low noise analog front-end (AFE)
NOISE FLOOR < 0.8 µV RMS Evaluated across 0.5 Hz – 45.0 Hz passband

DETAILED HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS

Signal Acquisition & Physical Characteristics

Subsystem Technical Parameter Operational Specification Design Rationale
Electrode Interface Electrode Type Ag/AgCl sintered active dry/wet compatible contacts Maintains stable skin-electrode contact without aggressive abrasion
Active Shielding Driven Right Leg (DRL) + Guarding Coaxial driven shields integrated along each electrode lead Suppresses 50/60Hz mains interference and ambient cable movement artifacts
Bandwidth & Filtering Hardware Passband 0.1 Hz – 250 Hz hardware passband; 50/60 Hz notch filter Preserves slow cortical potentials and articulatory beta-frequency bursts
Telemetry & Transmission Wireless & Wired Modes Bluetooth 5.2 Low Energy (BLE) / USB-C Direct Serial Enables both ambulatory data collection and ultra-low-latency tethered recording
Power & Autonomy Lithium Polymer Battery Continuous 6-hour operation on full charge (USB-C rechargable) Supports multi-session research protocols without mid-session interruption
