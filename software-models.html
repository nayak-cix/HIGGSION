SOFTWARE SUBCATEGORY 03

NEURAL MODELS &
APPLICATIONS

Investigative deep learning architectures decoding non-invasive EEG into articulatory phonemes and digital intent, designed to restore communication pathways for individuals experiencing locked-in states or speech motor loss.

RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Conformer-128 Spatiotemporal Backbone

Unified convolution and self-attention blocks extracting local temporal patterns alongside cross-electrode spatial co-activations across a 400ms sliding context window.

EXPLORATORY MODEL

Probabilistic Phoneme Estimation

Continuous projection of neural embeddings into probability distributions over 42 English phoneme classes, calibrated to handle acoustic-temporal ambiguity directly.

CONCEPT PROTOCOL

Constrained Beam Search Decoder

Transformer-assisted and Weighted Finite-State Transducer (WFST) language restraint algorithms to curb stochastic hallucinations and synthesize deterministic words.

Factual & Clinical Disclaimer

All decoding benchmarks reflect laboratory R&D configurations under controlled trials. BrainWave models are investigational prototypes and do not constitute approved medical devices for diagnostic or clinical speech replacement.
