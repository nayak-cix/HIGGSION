BrainWave is a neurotechnology initiative bridging the gap between human neural dynamics and intelligent computing.

We develop non-invasive interfaces that decode neural patterns to enable natural assistive communication, advance neurological research, and pioneer non-invasive brain–computer interactions for individuals facing motor impairments.

01

Neural Intent Capture

Isolating covert speech patterns and articulatory motor intent before signal attenuation.

02

Low-Noise Instrumentation

Active differential shielding delivering sub-microvolt resolution across scalp montages.

03

Translational Vectors

Building assistive pathways for locked-in states, stroke rehabilitation, and ALS.

Neurological Challenges

I

ALS (Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis)

A progressive disease that weakens muscles and can eventually deprive patients of their ability to speak or move.

II

Locked-In Syndrome

A condition in which patients remain conscious but lose nearly all voluntary muscle movement, making communication extremely difficult.

III

Stroke

Brain damage caused by interrupted blood supply or bleeding that can impair speech, movement, and cognitive function.

IV

Spinal Cord Injury

Damage to the spinal cord that can disrupt movement, sensation, and communication between the brain and body.

V

Cerebral Palsy

A group of disorders affecting movement, muscle coordination, and posture due to early brain development or injury.

VI

Epilepsy

A neurological disorder characterized by recurrent seizures caused by abnormal electrical activity in the brain.

VII

Parkinson's Disease

A progressive disorder that affects movement, often causing tremors, stiffness, and slowed movements.

VIII

Essential Tremor

A neurological condition that causes involuntary shaking, particularly during activities such as writing or holding objects.

IX

Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)

Brain damage resulting from an external force that may affect movement, memory, communication, or consciousness.

X

Disorders of Consciousness

Conditions in which brain injury or illness severely reduces awareness of oneself or the surrounding environment.

XI

Sleep Disorders

Conditions that disrupt normal sleep patterns and may affect brain function, alertness, and overall health.

XII

Severe Motor and Communication Impairments

Conditions that limit a person's ability to move or communicate through conventional speech and physical interaction.
